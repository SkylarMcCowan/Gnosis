## 3. Core mechanisms

Core mechanisms

The party employs a robust system of internal discipline to maintain control over its members and supporters. The party's members are often subjected to intensive training and indoctrination, which aims to instill a sense of duty and service to the party.

The Golden Dawn also utilizes a well-crafted propaganda campaign to maintain its influence and control. This includes the use of radical nationalist rhetoric to appeal to Greek nationalist sentiments, as well as the promotion of anti-Semitic and anti-immigrant narratives. The party's leaders and supporters are trained to use these narratives effectively, to mobilize public opinion and consolidate the party's power.

Tactical political maneuvers are another key mechanism of the Golden Dawn's operation. The party has been adept at navigating the Greek political landscape, securing representation in both the Greek Parliament and local administrations. This has been achieved through a combination of radical political stances, tactical electoral strategies, and the use of party machinery to mobilize its supporters.

The party's leadership is also skilled in the art of negotiation and compromise, particularly when it comes to securing government contracts and other lucrative opportunities. The Golden Dawn's leaders are often willing to engage in strategic alliances and power-sharing arrangements, which serve to maintain the party's influence and control over the Greek state apparatus.

In conclusion, the Golden Dawn's core mechanisms are a complex and multifaceted set of strategies, designed to maintain its power and influence over Greek politics and society. These mechanisms draw upon ideological discipline, psychological manipulation, tactical political maneuvers, and a highly organized and disciplined party structure. Despite facing significant opposition and legal challenges, the party continues to be a formidable force in Greek politics.
